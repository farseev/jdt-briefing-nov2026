JDT academy pathway: from JDT V to the first team

How the JDT academy works: teams from Under-12 to Under-23, JDT II to JDT V, and the route young Johor players take to the Southern Tigers first team.

See the JDT team pages

In short

The JDT academy is the club's youth system, with teams from Under-12 to Under-23 and senior reserve sides JDT II to JDT V. It is the only Malaysian club academy with AFC Two-Star Academy Accreditation, and it supplies players to Malaysia's age-group national teams.

Who it is for

  • Johor families with a football-playing child
  • Fans who want to follow the next generation before they reach the first team
  • Schools and coaches looking for a clear professional route

What it solves

  • Talented young players in Johor often lack visibility and a clear route up.
  • Parents rarely know how trials, age groups and the reserve teams connect.
  • Fans only meet young players once they debut, and miss the journey that got them there.

What JDT offers

  • Age-group teams from Under-12 to Under-23
  • Reserve and development sides JDT II, JDT III, JDT IV and JDT V
  • AFC Two-Star Academy Accreditation, the only one held by a Malaysian club academy
  • A track record of supplying national age-group squads: 15 of 23 players in Malaysia's 2026 ASEAN U-19 squad

Questions

How many age groups does the JDT academy have?

According to the club's academy leadership, JDT runs the only fully professional Malaysian club academy, with teams from Under-12 up to Under-23. Above the academy sit the senior development sides JDT II, JDT III, JDT IV and JDT V, each with its own roster on the club website.

Is the JDT academy accredited?

Yes. JDT is the only Malaysian club academy that holds AFC Two-Star Academy Accreditation from the Asian Football Confederation, which recognises a structured and continuous approach to player development across age groups.

Do JDT academy players play for Malaysia?

Yes. When Malaysia named its 23-player squad for the 2026 ASEAN Under-19 Championship, 15 players came from JDT: eight from JDT III and seven from JDT IV, according to the Football Association of Malaysia's announcement reported by Bernama.

How can my child get a trial at JDT?

Trial dates and requirements are announced by the club. Follow the official JDT website news section and the club's social channels for academy announcements, and contact the club through its official contact page for current trial information.
