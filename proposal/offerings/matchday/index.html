JDT matchday guide: tickets, gates and kick-off times

Where to buy official JDT tickets, where to check fixtures and kick-off times, and how to plan your arrival at Sultan Ibrahim Stadium on matchday.

Buy tickets on Tickethotline

In short

Buy JDT tickets through the club's official ticketing partner Tickethotline, check fixtures and kick-off times on the official JDT schedule page, and read the ground regulations before travelling to Sultan Ibrahim Stadium. Big matches can sell out online.

Who it is for

  • Regular supporters planning every home match
  • First-time visitors and away fans
  • Groups and families coordinating travel

What it solves

  • Fans miss lineups, kick-off changes and ticket windows.
  • Information is spread across many posts and group chats.
  • High-demand matches sell out, and unofficial sellers add risk.

What JDT offers

  • Official tickets through Tickethotline
  • Fixtures, results and standings on the official JDT website
  • Ground regulations published by the club
  • Sold-out notices for high-demand matches, such as the 2026 Charity Shield

Questions

Where can I buy official JDT tickets?

JDT match tickets are sold through Tickethotline (tickethotline.com.my), the ticketing platform the club uses for home fixtures and special matches such as the 2026 friendly against Chelsea. Buying from the official channel avoids counterfeit or resale risk.

Where do I check JDT fixtures and kick-off times?

The official JDT website lists the Southern Tigers' schedule, results and league standings. Kick-off times can change for broadcast or continental fixtures, so check the schedule page and the club's social channels again on the day of the match.

Do JDT matches sell out?

Yes, high-demand matches do. The 2026 Charity Shield against Selangor FC sold out online, according to The Iskandarian, and continental nights draw some of the biggest crowds of the season. Buy early for big fixtures.

What are the stadium rules?

The club publishes ground regulations for Sultan Ibrahim Stadium on its official website. Read them before travelling, as they cover what supporters may bring into the stadium and expected conduct on matchday.
