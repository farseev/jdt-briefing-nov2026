Is a JDT match a good family day out in Johor?

Yes. A JDT match at Sultan Ibrahim Stadium works well as a family day out: the 40,000-seat stadium opened in 2020, has prayer rooms, and big matches have featured local Johor food. Arrive early, buy official tickets and agree a meeting point with children.

Football as a Johor family tradition

For many Johor families, supporting the Southern Tigers is something passed down: a grandparent who followed the team on the radio, a parent who took them to their first match, a child now learning the chants. The stadium has changed, but that shared ritual is what a matchday can still offer: one afternoon or evening when three generations sit in the same stand.

When JDT hosted Buriram United in the AFC Champions League Elite in March 2025, Bernama reported that more than 20,000 fans were expected and that local dishes such as bubur lambuk and mee kawah would be ready for them at the stadium. It is a small detail, but it shows how a big Asian night can still feel like a Johor gathering.

What makes Sultan Ibrahim Stadium family-friendly?

  • Space. The stadium opened in 2020 and holds 40,000 fans, as Chelsea FC noted when announcing its 2026 visit.
  • Facilities. Bernama reported that the stadium has modern amenities including prayer rooms.
  • Premium options. Before the 2025-26 season the club added 12 corporate boxes and two premium lounges, according to Malay Mail, which suit larger family groups or celebrations.

How do you make it a tradition rather than a one-off?

Families that come back tend to build small rituals around the match: the same meal before kick-off, the same seats, a photo in the same spot, a chant the youngest learns first. Festive periods are a natural place to start. A match around Christmas, New Year or Chinese New Year gives the extended family a reason to come together, and the stadium becomes part of how the holiday is spent.

Club merchandise is part of it too. Official shirts and scarves are sold through the JDT online store, so the whole family can turn up in colours.

Food, prayer and comfort: practical tips for older family members

Grandparents often enjoy the occasion most and find the logistics hardest. A few small choices help. Choose seats closer to the entrance you will use, so the walk is shorter. Agree on a quieter moment, such as before the main crowd arrives, to find food and use facilities. Bring what the ground regulations allow for comfort, and plan prayer times around kick-off, since the stadium has prayer rooms.

For young children, ear protection for loud moments, a simple plan if someone gets separated, and a named meeting point make the day calmer. Many families take a photo of the children in their shirts before leaving home, so stewards can be shown what they are wearing if needed.

A simple family matchday plan

  1. Choose the match. League matches are often easier for a first visit than sold-out cup finals or continental nights.
  2. Buy together. Book all seats in one purchase on the official platform so the family sits together.
  3. Eat local. Plan a Johor meal before or after the match and make it part of the ritual.
  4. Learn one chant. Teach the youngest one chant before you go; it turns watching into taking part.
  5. Come back. Pick the next match before you leave, so the outing becomes a habit.

Is it worth it for visitors to Johor?

For visitors, a JDT home match is one of the most direct ways to see modern Johor: a club that has won 12 straight league titles, playing in one of Southeast Asia's best-known stadiums, with a crowd that mixes families, students and lifelong supporters. Clubs from around the region now bring their own supporters to Iskandar Puteri for continental matches.

Why matchdays matter to Johor

A full stadium is more than a crowd. On a big night, Iskandar Puteri fills with families, students, food sellers and visiting fans from across Asia, and the city gets a taste of what a continental club means for Johor. Families who make the match a regular outing are part of that atmosphere every week.

Frequently asked

How big is Sultan Ibrahim Stadium?

Sultan Ibrahim Stadium holds 40,000 fans and opened in 2020, according to Chelsea FC's announcement of its 2026 pre-season match there and a Bernama report.

Does the stadium have prayer rooms?

Yes. Bernama reported in March 2025 that Sultan Ibrahim Stadium is equipped with modern amenities including prayer rooms for fans.

Can I bring my family to an AFC Champions League Elite match?

Yes, with an official ticket for each person. Continental matches are high-demand and can sell out, so buy early through Tickethotline.
